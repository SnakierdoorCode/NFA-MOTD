1Community & General Rules

  • No Disrespect / HarassmentDo not harass, grief, troll, or disrespect other players out-of-character (OOC).
  • Zero Tolerance for Hate SpeechRacist, sexist, homophobic, or derogatory slurs and symbols are strictly prohibited, even in roleplay.
  • No SpamDo not mic-spam, chat-spam, advert-spam, or job-spam.
  • No Exploits or CheatingDo not abuse map glitches, Lua exploits, third-party cheats, macros, or auto-clickers. Report bugs to staff.
  • No ImpersonationDo not copy another player’s name, a staff member’s name, or pretend to be staff.
  • No Scamming / DDoS ThreatsDo not scam players on trades or jobs. Any DDoS, doxxing, or hacking threats result in a permanent ban.
  • No False ReportsDo not submit troll or intentionally false @ reports.
  • Staff Decisions Are FinalDo not argue with staff during active sits. If you disagree with a punishment, appeal it on our Discord.

2Core Roleplay Rules

  • No RDM (Random Deathmatch)You may not kill or damage another player without a valid RP reason. Verbal disrespect or mic spam is not a valid reason to kill someone.
  • No NLR (New Life Rule)When you die, you forget your previous life and all events leading to your death. You must stay out of your red NLR zone for 3 minutes and cannot return to an active raid.
  • No FailRP / Job AbuseAct according to your job’s role. Do not switch jobs to give yourself weapons, printers, or shipments and immediately switch back.
  • No Spawn AbuseDo not arrest, mug, kidnap, build, or shoot inside or into spawn. Do not run into spawn to escape an active RP situation.
  • No MetagamingDo not use OOC chat, Discord calls, or spectator info to gain an unfair advantage in RP.
  • Proper Adverts OnlyDo not use /advert for OOC talk. Do not stack or chain adverts (e.g., Raid/Assist/Mug/Over in one line).
  • No Body Blocking / Door SpamDo not block doorways with your body or spam E on doors to prevent players from entering or leaving.
  • Name Change CooldownDo not change your RP name more than 3 times within 30 minutes.

3Raiding, Mugging & Kidnapping

  • Advert RequirementsYou must /advert Raid before lockpicking, keypad cracking, or breaching a base, and /advert Over when finished.
  • Raid Duration & CooldownRaids may last up to 10 minutes. You must wait 20 minutes before raiding the same player, base, or PD again.
  • No Chain RaidingYou may not assist another group in raiding a base you recently raided to bypass your 20-minute cooldown.
  • No Random Killing During RaidsYou may only kill players inside the building being raided or armed defenders actively shooting at you from outside.
  • Mugging RulesOnly criminal/raiding jobs may mug. You must /advert Mug with a maximum demand of $5,000 and give the victim at least 10 seconds to comply before shooting. You cannot mug the same player within 15 minutes.
  • Kidnapping RulesOnly designated kidnapping jobs may kidnap. Maximum ransom is $10,000, and you may not hold a hostage for more than 10 minutes.

4Building & Basing Rules

  • No Prop AbuseNo prop climbing, prop surfing, prop pushing, prop killing, or prop blocking public areas and unowned buildings.
  • No Street BuildingDo not build in the streets or on sidewalks unless your job explicitly allows it (e.g., Hobo or Gun Dealer stand).
  • Fading Door & Keypad Rules
    • Every fading door must have at least 2 working keypads/buttons (one on the outside, one on the inside) placed visibly right next to the door.
    • Minimum keypad hold length is 4 seconds.
    • Maximum of 3 fading doors per entrance path into your base.
    • No Fading Door Abuse (FDA): Do not use keyboard binds to rapidly open/close fading doors to shoot through them.
  • No Crouch / Maze / One-Way Bases
    • Players must be able to walk upright through your entire base. Never force raiders to crouch or jump.
    • No pitch-black bases, invisible/no-collided false walls, or pixel-peeking shooting slits.
  • KOS SignsKill-On-Sight (KOS) signs are only valid for the interior of your owned base. You cannot set a KOS line on a public sidewalk or street.

5Job-Specific Rules

  • Civil Protection / Police / Mayor
    • Government jobs may not own money printers, grow drugs, or raid without a valid warrant.
    • Random arrests (RDA) and random weapon checks are prohibited.
    • Mayors may not create “minge laws” (e.g., “Walking on sidewalks is KOS”). All laws must be reasonable.
  • Gun Dealers / Black Market Dealers
    • You must own a public shop and sell to customers. Do not self-supply and switch jobs.
  • Hitmen
    • You may only accept hits through the hit menu and must not advertise hits in /advert.

Reporting Rulebreakers

Contact an available admin on this server and let them know.

Use @ before typing a chat message to send it to admins.

If no admin is available, note the player’s name and the current time, then let an admin know as soon as they are available.
